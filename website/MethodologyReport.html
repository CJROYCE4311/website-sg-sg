SG@SG WHS Review Methodology

Purpose: give the committee a tournament-by-tournament view of whether a player's gross score, when converted through rating and slope, materially outperformed their Handicap Index.

This methodology is now the primary internal review model. The older best-3/best-6 handicap audit and handicap detail pages have been archived for reference; they are no longer part of the live admin dashboard.

Open WHS Exceptional Score Review

Primary Tournament Round Method

1. Convert Gross Score to Target Differential

Use Sterling Grove rating and slope so every tournament round is measured against course difficulty, not only par.

(113 / slope) * (gross score - course rating)

2. Compare to Handicap Index

Measure how many strokes better or worse the round was than the player's potential.

Handicap Index - Target Differential

3. Probability and Flagging

The report uses the USGA Appendix E Exceptional Tournament Score Probability Table and flags any round that is 7.0 or more strokes better than Handicap Index.

0.0+ At or Better
3.0+ Great Round
5.0+ Elite Round
7.0+ Exceptional
