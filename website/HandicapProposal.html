SG@SG Fair Competition Review

Committee Review Framework for Tournament Equity

The SG@SG monthly golf tournament has grown remarkably from a small Saturday gathering to a popular event attracting over 40 players, with strong support from the club’s Director of Golf and Head Pro. The shotgun start format allows the field to finish together, followed by payouts and social time at Copper & Rye, fostering a vibrant community atmosphere.

However, as participation has increased, concerns about handicap fairness have emerged, particularly around the honor-based self-reporting of scores into the GHIN system, which forms the basis of official USGA Handicap Indexes.

To address perceived inequities while maintaining the spirit of the game, the admin team is now using a WHS-style tournament review as the primary internal screen. The goal is to identify statistically unusual tournament rounds before making any policy decision, not to automatically punish good play.

Methodology Overview

The current review approach evaluates every completed 2026-season SG@SG tournament round from November 2025 forward:

  1. Convert each gross score to a target differential: Use (113 / slope) * (gross score - course rating) so the round is judged against course difficulty rather than only net score to par.
  2. Compare the round to Handicap Index: Calculate Handicap Index - Target Differential. Positive values mean the player performed better than their index; negative values mean the round was worse than index.
  3. Assign probability context: Use the USGA Appendix E Exceptional Tournament Score Probability Table to show the approximate odds of that performance level for the player's Handicap Index band.
  4. Flag exceptional rounds: Use 7.0 strokes better than Handicap Index as the WHS exceptional-score threshold.
    • Exceptional flags are committee review prompts, not automatic adjustments.
    • Non-flagged low net scores can still win money while remaining statistically ordinary for the player’s index band.

Interactive Dashboard

The new internal dashboard displays:

  • Only completed 2026-season tournaments, beginning November 2025
  • Newest tournament first
  • All players in each tournament, sorted from best net score to worst net score
  • Gross score, course handicap, net score, Handicap Index, target differential, strokes better or worse than index, and probability
  • Clear flags for great, elite, and WHS exceptional-score rounds

The previous best-3/best-6 handicap audit and handicap detail pages have been archived. They remain available as historical references, but they are no longer the active committee dashboard.

Any future tournament-specific adjustment should be based on repeated evidence, committee review, and transparent criteria. The current WHS report is a diagnostic tool for fairness discussions, not an automatic penalty system.
