Calculation Guide

The SG@SG Report Formula:

Score Differential - Course Handicap = Gap

Differential vs. Net to Par:

While "Net to Par" is common for tournament scoring, it does not account for course difficulty. Score Differential measures performance relative to the Course Rating (the score a scratch golfer is expected to shoot), not the course Par.

Because Course Ratings often differ from Par, the Score Differential provides the only statistically consistent baseline for calculating scoring probability across different courses.
